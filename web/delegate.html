Connecting here does not sign you in, and delegating is not a token approval

Not a login

Connecting only shows QuoPull your wallet address. There is no account, no password, and no session. You can disconnect from the address button at the top, or in your wallet, whenever you want.

No other site

Everything happens on this page. It never sends you to delegate.xyz and runs none of their code. It talks only to your wallet and to the chain.

Not a token approval

Yes, delegating is a transaction you approve in your wallet, so it can feel like a token approval. It is not one. It moves no funds, grants no access to your tokens or NFTs, and gives QuoPull no custody of anything. It writes one scoped permission to the neutral delegate.xyz registry (not to QuoPull): that wallet B may mint your allocation, on this contract only. You can revoke it anytime.

Already delegated everything to a wallet?

Then it can already mint for you, without signing anything here. delegate.xyz has a blanket "delegate all" option and the mint accepts it, so holders who already use one are not locked out. If you would rather it could not mint your allocation, revoke it at delegate.xyz first.

Four passes is the jackpot line

The guaranteed allowlist allocation is 3 passes. The weekly holder jackpot counts only wallets holding 4 or more, so the guaranteed amount alone is one short of it. Top up in the Top-Up window or later. Free daily entries work from your first pass.

Daily raffleEvery pass earns free entries every day. Rarity sets the entry weight above: how many draw seats a pass competes for, not a fixed share of any prize.
Holder drawWeekly. Five passes are drawn at random from the whole range and each one pays an equal fifth of the pot in $QUOTRON. It picks passes, not wallets, so more eligible passes means proportionally more chances, and one wallet can win several of the five.
Hold 4The holder draw only counts wallets holding 4 or more passes, held without a break from the week's freeze until the draw runs, about 4.5 to 7 days later. Dropping below 4 loses that week, and buying back does not restore it. The guaranteed allowlist allocation was 3, one short.
